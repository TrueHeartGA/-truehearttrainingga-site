WELCOME

A Message From Our Institution

Welcome to True Heart Training GA. We appreciate your interest in pursuing healthcare education with our institution. Our goal is to provide practical instruction, supportive learning experiences, and career-focused training that prepares students for opportunities in healthcare.

We believe education is an important investment in your future. We are committed to helping students develop knowledge, confidence, professionalism, and compassion.

Our Mission

True Heart Training GA is committed to providing accessible, high-quality healthcare education that prepares individuals with the knowledge, practical skills, and confidence needed to succeed in today's healthcare workforce.

Through hands-on instruction, supportive learning environments, and career-focused training, we strive to develop competent, compassionate, and professional healthcare workers.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a recognized leader in healthcare education and workforce development throughout Georgia, creating opportunities for individuals to build meaningful careers while strengthening the communities and healthcare organizations we serve.

ACADEMIC PROGRAMS

Programs of Study

True Heart Training GA offers healthcare education and professional development opportunities designed to support different learning and career goals.

CNA Training Program

A six-week nursing assistant training program combining classroom instruction and practical skills preparation.

  • Program length: 6 weeks
  • Schedule: Monday–Thursday
  • Hours: 9:00 AM–3:30 PM
  • Tuition: $1,250
  • Enrollment fee: $75

Georgia Medication Aide (Med Tech)

A focused four-day medication aide training program for eligible Georgia nursing assistants.

  • Program length: 4 days
  • Tuition: $375
  • Must be a CNA in Georgia
  • Must have no adverse findings on the Georgia Nurse Aide Registry

Personal Care Aide (PCA)

A 40-hour introductory training program designed for individuals interested in exploring employment opportunities in home healthcare.

  • Program length: 40 hours
  • Five consecutive training days
  • Tuition: $200
  • Certificate of Completion upon successful completion

This is a non-certification training program.

CPR & BLS Training

American Heart Association training opportunities available by appointment.

  • BLS Provider
  • Heartsaver CPR/AED
  • First Aid
  • Bloodborne Pathogens

Tuesday–Friday by appointment, with select Saturday classes.

ADMISSIONS

Enrollment Information

Admissions Process

Prospective students must complete the enrollment process for their selected program and provide required documentation.

Admission qualifications and documentation requirements vary by training program.

Tuition & Payment Options

Tuition varies by program. Students should review the published program information before enrolling.

Weekly and biweekly payment plans are available for the CNA program.

Contact the school for current payment arrangements and applicable fees.

STUDENT RESPONSIBILITIES

Academic Expectations

Attendance & Participation

Students are expected to attend scheduled classes, arrive on time, participate in learning activities, and complete required coursework.

Professional Conduct

Students are expected to demonstrate respect, integrity, accountability, professionalism, and compassion throughout their training.

Skills & Competency

Students must complete the applicable academic and practical skills requirements established for their individual program.

Program Completion

Completion requirements vary by program. Students must successfully meet the applicable attendance, coursework, skills, and evaluation requirements.
