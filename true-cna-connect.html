Welcome to True CNA Connect

True CNA Connect is a caregiver referral network created to connect qualified CNAs and PCAs with employment opportunities and caregiver needs.

Whether you're a caregiver looking for your next opportunity, a home-care company looking for dependable candidates, or a family looking for caregiver assistance, we're here to help make the connection.

Why True CNA Connect?

  • Built through True Heart Training GA's healthcare education network.
  • Focused specifically on CNAs and PCAs.
  • Connects caregivers with employment opportunities.
  • Helps home-care companies identify caregiver candidates.
  • Creates a pathway for trained caregivers to find opportunities.
  • Designed to make the referral process organized and professional.

Important: True CNA Connect is a caregiver referral network operated by True Heart Training GA. Participation does not guarantee employment, placement, availability, or hiring. Employers and referral recipients are responsible for their own screening, credential verification, background checks, references, interviews, hiring decisions, and applicable employment requirements. Services are subject to applicable law and applicable referral agreements.
