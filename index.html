ABOUT TRUE HEART TRAINING GA

Training With Purpose. Education With Heart.

True Heart Training GA is a healthcare education school in Covington, Georgia, focused on practical, career-minded training. We create a supportive learning environment where students can build knowledge, strengthen hands-on skills and prepare for opportunities in the healthcare field. We also provide training solutions for businesses and organizations that need CPR, BLS and other healthcare-related education.
