ABOUT TRUE HEART TRAINING GA

Education With Purpose. Training With Heart.

True Heart Training GA is a healthcare education school in Covington, Georgia, committed to helping students build practical skills and confidence for the healthcare field.

Our approach combines supportive instruction, practical preparation and student-focused guidance. Whether you're beginning your healthcare career or adding new skills, we're here to help you take the next step.

TRUE HEART TRAINING GA

Our Institutional Foundation

Building stronger communities through education, professional development, and quality healthcare training.

Our Mission

True Heart Training GA is committed to providing accessible, high-quality healthcare education that prepares individuals with the knowledge, practical skills, and confidence needed to succeed in today's healthcare workforce.

Through hands-on instruction, supportive learning environments, and career-focused training, we strive to develop competent, compassionate, and professional healthcare workers.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a recognized leader in healthcare education and workforce development throughout Georgia, creating opportunities for individuals to build meaningful careers while strengthening the communities and healthcare organizations we serve.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide our institution and everything we do.

Excellence

Maintaining high educational standards.

Integrity

Leading with honesty and accountability.

Compassion

Teaching with empathy, respect, and understanding.

Professionalism

Preparing students for professional healthcare environments.

Student Success

Supporting students throughout their educational journey.

Our Educational Philosophy

We believe that quality healthcare education goes beyond textbooks and classroom instruction. Students learn best through a combination of knowledge, hands-on experiences, professional guidance, and compassionate leadership. Our goal is to help every student develop the confidence and skills needed to make a positive difference in healthcare.

ACADEMIC INFORMATION

Student Resources & Academic Policies

At True Heart Training GA, we are committed to providing students with the guidance, resources, and expectations needed for a successful educational experience.

Admissions Information

We welcome individuals interested in pursuing healthcare education and developing professional skills. Students must complete the enrollment process for their selected training program.

Enrollment Requirements

Enrollment requirements vary by program. Applicants must provide the documentation, qualifications, and information required for their selected course.

Student Expectations

Students are expected to demonstrate professionalism, respect, integrity, accountability, and appropriate conduct throughout their training.

Attendance & Participation

Regular attendance, punctuality, classroom participation, and completion of assigned coursework are important parts of the student learning experience. Attendance requirements vary by program.

Student Support Services

Our team is committed to providing educational guidance, communication, and support to help students navigate their training experience.

Program Completion & Graduation

Students must successfully meet the academic, attendance, skills, and other applicable requirements established for their individual training program.
