FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Need to Know Before You Register?

How do I register?

Click any Register Now button to open our online registration form. Complete the form and follow the enrollment instructions.

Where are classes held?

True Heart Training GA is located at 2624 Highway 212, Covington, Georgia 30016.

How do I access my online class?

PCA and Med Tech students can use the Student Portal to go directly to their Google Classroom.

Can you train businesses or organizations?

Yes. We provide CPR/BLS and other training solutions for businesses, daycare programs and organizations.

How can I get help with registration?

Call 470-639-9498 or email truehearttrainingga@gmail.com for enrollment assistance.

Do class dates change?

Class schedules can change as enrollment periods are updated. Check the Upcoming Classes section for the latest dates.
